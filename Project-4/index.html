HIS LIFE STORY

Biography

Imran Ahmed Khan Niazi was born on 5 October 1952 in Lahore, Pakistan. He received his early education in Lahore and later studied at Keble College, University of Oxford. From a young age, he developed a strong interest in cricket and went on to represent Pakistan at the international level.

During his cricket career, Imran Khan became one of Pakistan's prominent all-rounders. He was appointed captain of the national cricket team in 1982. His most recognized sporting achievement came in 1992, when he led Pakistan to victory in the Cricket World Cup. He retired from international cricket after that year.

After his cricket career, he became involved in charitable work. Inspired by his mother Shaukat Khanum's experience with cancer, he helped establish the Shaukat Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital and Research Centre in Lahore, which opened in December 1994. He also became associated with educational projects, including Namal Institute.

In April 1996, Imran Khan founded the political party Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI). Following years of political activity and election campaigns, he became Prime Minister of Pakistan on 18 August 2018. He served in that office until April 2022. His public career includes both his time in sports and his involvement in Pakistan's political history.
